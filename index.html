ABOUT THE CLUB

Thinking beyond the textbook.

Homeoeconomicus is a student-driven economics and interdisciplinary platform at IISER Tirupati.

We bring together economics, statistics, mathematics, data science, research, behavioural science and public policy to understand the world around us.
